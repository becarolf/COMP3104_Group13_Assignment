George Brown College is a public college of applied arts and technology in Toronto, Ontario.
Founded in 1967, the college offers certificate, diploma, degree, and continuing education programs.

The college has three main campuses: Casa Loma, St. James, and Waterfront.
Its programs combine classroom learning with practical projects and opportunities to build skills for the workplace.

Applied Learning and Workplace Skills
Applied learning gives students opportunities to use course concepts in practical assignments and projects.
This can help build problem-solving, communication, and teamwork skills alongside subject knowledge.
These skills are useful in the workplace, where people often need to explain their work,
coordinate with others, and adapt when requirements change.

Student Support and Learning Resources

Students can use college learning resources and support services to help with their studies and progress toward their goals.
Knowing what resources are available and asking for help when needed can make it easier to manage coursework, strengthen
skills, and prepare for future work.

Learning in Toronto

George Brown College's Toronto setting gives students a chance to learn in a large and diverse city. Toronto includes
organizations and employers from many fields, which can help students connect classroom topics to real industries and
career paths. Learning about the city's communities and workplaces can also help students build a broader perspective.
